You are a careful visual reasoner. You will be given a question about an
image, the gold answer, and 16 independent reasoning attempts from a
vision-language model. Each attempt includes:
  - object grounding: bounding points the model attended to, in the form
    <objN>description [x, y]</objN>
  - the model's reasoning trace
  - the answer it produced

Your task: produce ONE clean, forward-reasoning trace that lands on the
gold answer, grounded in the visual evidence the candidates collectively
identify.

================================================================
QUESTION:
{question}

GOLD ANSWER:
{gold_answer}

CANDIDATES (16 independent attempts):
{candidates_block}
================================================================

How to reason — work through this in order:

1. Cross-candidate visual consensus. Identify the objects and grounding
   points that appear in MULTIPLE candidates (same description, similar
   coordinates). This is your reliable visual prior. Treat objects or
   spatial claims that show up in only one candidate as suspect — do
   not build the trace on them.

2. Spatial structure. From the consensus grounding points, infer the
   spatial relationships that matter for the question (relative depth,
   left/right, in-front-of, contained-in, distance from camera, etc.).
   Cite the grounded objects by their description and approximate
   location, e.g. "the construction vehicle near [750, 400]".

3. Step-by-step derivation. Reason from the consensus visual evidence
   to the answer in a small number of explicit steps. Each step should
   follow from the previous. Do not skip from observation to conclusion.

4. Forward reasoning only. The trace must read as if you derived the
   answer from the image. Never write "since the answer is", "given the
   gold answer", "working backward", "we know the answer", or any
   phrasing that reveals you knew the answer in advance.

5. Honesty gate. If the candidate consensus genuinely contradicts the
   gold answer and you cannot construct an honest forward-reasoning
   trace from the visual evidence that lands on the gold answer, output
   <answer>REJECT</answer> and use <think> to briefly explain why.

================================================================
OUTPUT FORMAT — exactly this, nothing else:

<think>
your step-by-step reasoning, citing grounded objects by description and
location
</think>
<answer>
the final answer on a single line; must match the gold answer's meaning
</answer>

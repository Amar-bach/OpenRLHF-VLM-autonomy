You will improve a vision-language reasoning example. The image was already
processed by a vision model and is summarized through 16 candidate reasoning
traces below — treat visual claims that recur across multiple candidates as
reliable, and treat single-candidate-only claims as suspect. Your job is to
produce ONE clean, forward-reasoning chain of thought that a student model
can learn from.

================================================================
QUESTION:
{question}

GOLD ANSWER:
{gold_answer}

CANDIDATES (presented in randomized order):
{candidates_block}
================================================================

SCORING RUBRIC (0-4 per dimension, with anchors):

Faithfulness — does the trace use only facts grounded in cross-candidate
consensus or the question itself?
  0 = contradicts consensus or invents visual facts
  2 = mostly grounded, minor unsupported claims
  4 = every visual claim is supported by multiple candidates

Logical validity — do steps follow from prior steps?
  0 = contains a logical fallacy or contradiction
  2 = mostly valid, one minor gap
  4 = every step follows cleanly

Completeness — could a reader verify the answer from the trace alone?
  0 = major hand-waving, missing key steps
  2 = adequate, a step or two left implicit
  4 = thorough, every necessary step is shown

Conciseness — is the trace free of redundancy and padding?
  0 = bloated, repetitive
  2 = acceptable, some filler
  4 = tight, no waste

================================================================
STRUCTURED REASONING PROCESS

Work through the following steps IN ORDER. Mirror each "Step N:" header
verbatim in your response so the structure is visible. Reason in prose
for Steps 1-4. Emit JSON only at Step 5.

----------------------------------------------------------------
Step 1: TRIAGE THE 16 CANDIDATES
For each candidate (in the order shown), write one sentence stating:
  (a) does its final answer match the gold answer?
  (b) what is the single biggest strength or weakness of its reasoning?

----------------------------------------------------------------
Step 2: CHOOSE AN ENRICHMENT STRATEGY
Pick exactly one of:
  refine     — one candidate is mostly correct; polish it
  rewrite    — no single candidate is clean, but useful steps are
               scattered across several; assemble from those parts
  synthesize — most candidates land on the correct answer but no
               single trace is salvageable; write fresh from
               cross-candidate visual consensus
  reject     — candidates collectively give no signal that the
               problem is solvable; do not emit an enriched trace

Write 1-2 sentences justifying the choice. If "refine", name the chosen
orig_idx.

----------------------------------------------------------------
Step 3: DERIVE THE ENRICHED CHAIN OF THOUGHT
Write the trace a student model will learn from. Hard rules:
  - Reads as forward reasoning. Do NOT mention the gold answer. Do NOT

  - Cite only visual facts that appear in multiple candidates, plus

  - Match the problem's complexity — neither hand-wave nor pad.
  - End with the final answer on its own line, prefixed "Answer: ".

(If Step 2 chose "reject", write only: "Skipped — strategy=reject.")

----------------------------------------------------------------
Step 4: SELF-CHECK
Restate the answer your Step 3 trace arrives at, then compare to the
gold answer. State explicitly: "Match: yes" or "Match: no — <why>".

----------------------------------------------------------------
Step 5: SCORE, GATE, AND EMIT JSON

================================================================
SCORING RUBRIC (1–5 per dim) — CALIBRATION IS STRICT.

A 5 means EXCEPTIONAL — top ~5% of traces. If you would say
"good" or "fine", that is a 4, not a 5. Default scores should be
3 or 4. Use 5 only when the trace is so clean it could not be
improved.

hallucination — visual claims fabricated from nothing:
  1 = many fabricated objects or relations not in image
  2 = one or two clear fabrications (e.g. invented an object)
  3 = one minor unsupported claim or vague reference to something
      that may or may not be in the image
  4 = nothing fabricated; every claim grounded; no embellishment
  5 = every claim verifiable in the image with precise reference;
      no hedging or filler that could be fabrication-prone

visual_grounding — per-<objN> description and coordinate accuracy:
  1 = most <objN> wrong (description or coords)
  2 = several <objN> wrong
  3 = one <objN> mildly off (small coord error or imprecise
      description) OR all <objN> correct but coords very rough
      (>50 units off)
  4 = all <objN> correct, coords approximate (within ~50 units)
  5 = all <objN> precisely accurate (descriptions exact, coords
      within ~20 units)

reasoning_quality — logical flow, no fallacies, conciseness:
  1 = incoherent, fallacious, or self-contradicting
  2 = a step does not follow; conclusion lands by luck
  3 = mostly sound, one weak step or noticeable padding
  4 = clean derivation, minor padding, every step a clear inference
  5 = every step a tight, necessary inference with zero redundancy;
      could not be shortened without losing information

REMINDER: Default to 3 or 4. A score of 5 in any dimension means
the trace is exceptional in that dimension. If the trace is merely
correct and clear, that is a 4.
